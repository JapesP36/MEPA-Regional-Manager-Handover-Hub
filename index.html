The Regional Manager role

The RM is the connective tissue between chapters, Area Directors, Regional leadership and EO Global—combining trusted relationships, judgement, data and execution.

01

Own chapter relationships

Be the primary staff partner to Presidents, Chapter Staff and ADs. Build trust before trying to change how a chapter works.

02

Protect chapter health

Use Chapter Health, leadership context and direct relationships to identify risk early and coordinate the right intervention.

03

Drive sustainable growth & retention

Connect GrowthCast, member experience and year-round retention management into chapter strategy—not separate campaigns.

04

Enable strategy & transitions

Support Annual / Mid-Year Strategy Summits, Long-Term Planning, President lifecycle and Chapter Staff transitions.

05

Connect Regional & Global

Translate policies and programmes into practical chapter actions, escalate issues, and bring field insights back into Regional/Global decision-making.

06

Run programmes & systems

Use dashboards, incentives, Monday.com, event plans and reporting frameworks to create visibility and follow-through without adding unnecessary admin.

Operating mindset: diagnose → partner → execute → evaluate → adapt. Dashboards should drive better conversations; systems should reduce friction and create capacity for relationship work.

Regional operating rhythm

Run the role on three overlapping rhythms: always-on chapter support, a weekly/monthly management cadence, and a July–June programme cycle.
